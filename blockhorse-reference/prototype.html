Horses, pegasi, unicorns and winged unicorns each have their own body shape, so there are four builds. Every one of the 260 horses uses the build for its kind and only the colours change. All four builds were searched and checked offline and are checked again here: every piece is joined to the base through its studs, nothing floats, nothing overlaps, and no larger piece hangs on a single stud. Models are computer-checked, not physically build-tested.

The side-view sprite becomes a 3D model 6 studs deep: four legs in two pairs, a 4-stud neck that rises out of the shoulder, two ears with the mane between them, below the ear tips, a 4-stud head, a 2-stud tail and mane crest, a 1-stud horn, and a 1-stud wing on each side of the back. The horn is centred on the 4-stud head with a jumper plate (one stud in the middle of a 1 × 2): the jumper and two plates make up the horn's first brick-height row, then each step above is held by a single stud. LEGO element IDs aren't in the bundled table for the jumper, so it is listed as BrickLink only. Every pixel row is one brick. A brick is 9.6 mm tall and a stud 8 mm wide, so the horse stands 20% taller than the sprite, with every row the same height. Two small changes make the sprite buildable: the horn is a staircase with steps two pixels long, and the three notches between the wing tips are filled, because pixels that only touch at a corner can't be held by studs.

Trait colours are CSS colour names in the original SVGs. Each is matched to the nearest brick colour with CIEDE2000, and traits that touch keep different brick colours when their original colours differ.
